## Champs

## Problem
Across African communities, cross-border mobile money (MoMo) transfers are slow, expensive, and fragmented, forcing people to rely on agents, informal brokers, or multiple platforms to send money across countries.
This creates high fees, delays, lack of transparency, and exclusion, especially for small remittances between everyday users.

## MVP
### Scope
A Platform leveraging blockchain technology and stablecoins to deliver a fast, low-cost, and inclusive remittance platform.

### Cardano Integration
Cardano is used as the backbone for:
AfrixPay uses Cardano as the blockchain settlement layer to move value instantly and securely between countries, while users interact only with familiar mobile money (MoMo) experiences.

Cardano enables low-cost, transparent, and trust-minimized transfers, allowing AfrixPay to bridge Momo systems across borders without relying on expensive intermediaries. On-chain transactions provide verifiable settlement, while off-chain integrations handle local mobile money cash-in and cash-out.
