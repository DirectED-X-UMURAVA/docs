## **STAKEHOLDER MAP** 

Categorized List 

1. The Caretakers (Support Wellbeing): 
	1. Parents/Guardians: Sending allowance to students across borders. 
	
	2. "Chamas" (Savings Groups): Informal community groups where trust is high, but technology is low. 
	
	3. Diaspora Family: Relatives abroad trying to send support home without losing 15% to fees. 

2. The Emerging Leaders (Innovators): 
	1. Social Commerce Sellers: Youth selling shoes, data, and gadgets on WhatsApp/Instagram. 
	
	2. Crypto-Miners: The large community of Pi Network users and airdrop hunters looking for utility. 
	
	3. Freelancers: Digital workers earning in USD who need offramps. 

3. The Stakeholders (Institutions): 
	1. Mobile Money Agents: The physical gatekeepers of cash (M-Pesa/MTN agents). 
	
	2. Suppliers/Wholesalers: Merchants in other countries who demand secure payment. 
	
	3. Logistics Providers: Bike riders and delivery services (who could act as verifiers). 

4. Affected Groups (The Victims of the Gap): 
	1. Unbanked Youth: Excluded from global commerce. 
	
	2. Cross-Border Traders: Losing margins to exchange rates and fraud.
