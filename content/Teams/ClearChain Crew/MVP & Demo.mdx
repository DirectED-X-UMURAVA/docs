# MVP & Demo
## Clear Chain Crew

### Problem
In our neighborhood and similar communities, tenants are often required to pay rent or deposits before houses are fully ready, while landlords sometimes receive money without any guarantee that tenants will respect the property or complete payments. Most rental agreements rely on verbal promises, paper receipts, or screenshots, which can easily be lost, changed, or denied. Because there is no neutral and transparent system to hold rent money, conflicts frequently arise over unfinished repairs, poor housing conditions, unpaid rent, or damaged property.

This situation leads to delays, disputes, stress, and financial losses for both tenants and landlords. Your team identified that the main issue is not just payment itself, but the absence of a trusted system that fairly protects both sides until all rental conditions are met

### MVP
#### Cardano Integration
Cardano is used as the main technology that makes our rent payment escrow system trustworthy and secure. In our prototype, rent is sent to a Cardano smart contract instead of directly to the landlord, where the money is safely locked until both the tenant and landlord confirm that the agreed conditions are met. Cardano records all payments and approvals on the blockchain, creating clear and tamper-proof proof that reduces conflicts and misunderstandings. Its low fees, energy-efficient design, and global access make it practical and affordable, especially for use in African communities and beyond.
