# **Team Reflection Analysis**

**PERSPECTIVE SHIFTS**
**Initial Assumption → Reality Discovery**

**Original View** 
	- **Technical Problem** requiring technical solution
	- Focus on Sophisticated blockchain with advanced cryptography
	- Build the most technically advanced system
	- Patients as passive recipients

**Shifted Understanding**
	- **Trust Problem** that technology can help solve
	- Focus on accessible, human-centered solution
	- Build most **practical useful** system
	- Patients as **active stakeholders** wanting agency


**Pivotal Moment: Marie's Insight**

"I don't need complicated technology. I need something my staff can use in 10 seconds while a customer is waiting." **— Marie, Pharmacy Owner, Kigali**

**Impact:** Completely reframed approach from technical excellence to practical utility

**Core Realization**
A **blockchain solution is only valuable if a rural health worker with basic smartphone literacy** can confidently use it.

**SURPRISES & EMOTIONAL DISCOVERIES**
**What They Expected to Find**

	• Business losses
	
	• Supply chain inefficiencies
	
	• Technical challenges

**What They Actually Found**
1. **Emotional Weight of the Challenge**
**Stories of:**

	• Parents watching children suffer
	
	• Healthcare workers feeling helpless
	
	• Families choosing between food and re-purchasing medications

**Insight:** Human cost is profound and immediate, not abstract

2. **Resilience and Hope**
Example: Dr. Uwase's handwritten ledger

	• Tracks suspected counterfeit cases
	
	• Maintains documentation despite no formal tools
	
	• Demonstrates proactive readiness for change

**Insight**: Community isn't resigned—they're actively seeking solutions

3. **Technology Readiness (Unexpected)**
Evidence in Rural Areas:

	• Mobile money usage
	
	• QR codes for COVID-19 tracking
	
	• Smartphone app familiarity

**Insight:** Infrastructure already exists in people's hands and habits—just need the right application
