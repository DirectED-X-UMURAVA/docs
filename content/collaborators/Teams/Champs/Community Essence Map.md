# **Community Essence Map**

**"The Stalled Handshake"** In walking through the market and speaking
with young online vendors, we observed a community that is hyperconnected digitally but economically disconnected by fear. We met
**"Mama Sarah,"** a boutique owner who sources clothes from
neighboring countries. She spends hours physically traveling to banks
because she is terrified to send mobile money to suppliers she hasn't met
face-to-face. We also met **"David,"** a university student who mines Pi
and does freelance design, but holds his wealth in volatile local currency
because he cannot easily access stablecoins.

**Key Observations & Patterns:**
**• The "Pay on Delivery" Trap:** Online sellers
(Instagram/WhatsApp) are struggling because buyers refuse to pay
upfront due to scam fears, but sellers are afraid to ship without
payment. Commerce is gridlocked.

**• The "Cash-Out" Anxiety:** Freelancers receiving crypto have to
go through 3-4 layers of "middlemen" to get cash, losing value at
every step.

• **Digital Wealth, Real-World Poverty:** Many youth have digital
assets (Pi, small crypto balances) but cannot use them to buy bread
or pay for transport.
